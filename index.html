1. Hardware & Laptop Equipment

How do I request an upgraded or replacement laptop?

GeneriCorp laptops are eligible for a standard hardware refresh every 36 months. If your current workstation is under 3 years old but experiencing chronic technical failures, manager approval is required prior to replacement.

  1. Log into the GeneriCorp IT Service Portal using your SSO credentials.
  2. Navigate to Service Catalog > Hardware Requests > Laptop Refresh.
  3. Select your standard tier profile: Standard Business Tier (Dell Latitude) or Developer/Creative Tier (MacBook Pro 16").
  4. Select your cost center manager for automatic workflow routing.
Delivery SLA: Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days domestically. Express 24-hour shipping is available for critical project emergencies with VP sign-off.
What should I do if my laptop screen breaks or physically damages?

All GeneriCorp hardware is covered under corporate Complete Care Accidental Damage Protection. Do not attempt self-repair or third-party repair shops, as this voids corporate warranties.

  • Immediately report the damage by creating a ticket under Incident > Accidental Damage Report.
  • If working at an office site, bring the damaged device directly to the IT Tech Bar on Floor 3 for a loaner exchange.
  • If remote, IT logistics will send a pre-padded return box with a prepaid FedEx label along with a temporary loaner unit.
How do I request a second monitor or desktop accessories?

Full-time remote and hybrid employees are entitled to standard ergonomic desktop peripherals funded by GeneriCorp IT support budgets.

Standard approved catalog items include:

  • Dual 27-inch 4K Dell Monitors & USB-C Universal Docking Station
  • Logitech HD Webcams & Wireless Headsets
  • DisplayPort to HDMI Adapters and Dongles

Submit a request via Service Catalog > Peripheral Procurement. Orders under $300 do not require line manager pre-approval.

How do I return my company-issued mobile phone or hardware?

When offboarding, transferring roles, or upgrading your corporate smartphone/hardware, all original equipment must be returned within 10 business days.

  1. Turn OFF Apple 'Find My' / Activation Lock or Google Device Protection before powering down the mobile device. (Critical step!)
  2. Backup any non-sensitive personal photos or contacts if allowed under compliance policy.
  3. Go to IT Portal > Asset Return Ticket to generate a prepaid UPS shipping label.
  4. Include device, charger, and docking hardware inside the return package.
What do I do if my laptop battery dies quickly or won't charge?

Batteries lose capacity over time. First, test your charger with another wall outlet and verify the power indicator light on the adapter cable.

  • Windows Users: Run powercfg /batteryreport in Command Prompt to generate a battery health diagnostic file.
  • Mac Users: Hold Option and click the Battery icon in your top menu bar to view battery status (Normal vs Service Recommended).

If health drops below 70%, open a ticket under Hardware > Battery Replacement Request for a free battery exchange.

How do I request an ergonomic keyboard, mouse, or desk setup?

GeneriCorp offers specialized ergonomic hardware (split keyboards, vertical mice, monitor riser arms) via our Ergonomic Support Initiative.

To request ergonomic gear:

  1. Complete the 10-minute online EHS Ergonomic Self-Assessment in Workday.
  2. Submit the assessment certificate attached to a ticket under Service Catalog > Ergonomic Peripherals.
  3. Hardware will be delivered to your assigned office desk or home address within 3 days.
How do I request a replacement charger or laptop dock?

Replacement 65W/130W USB-C chargers and Thunderbolt docking stations can be requested directly without manager approval in cases of loss or malfunction.

Visit IT Portal > Quick Replacement Catalog and specify whether you require standard USB-C Power Delivery, MagSafe 3 (Mac), or Dell Thunderbolt Docking Stations.

2. Webex & Collaboration Tools

How to install Webex on your workstation?

Cisco Webex is the primary enterprise video conferencing platform at GeneriCorp. Standard installations do not require local administrator rights.

  1. Open GeneriCorp Software Center (Windows) or Self Service App (macOS).
  2. Search for Cisco Webex Meetings & Messaging.
  3. Click Install. The process completes in 2 minutes.
  4. Launch Webex and log in using your full company email (e.g. username@genericorp.com). You will be redirected to Okta SSO for authentication.
Webex FAQ 1: How do I schedule a Webex meeting and add a personal room link in Outlook?

You can schedule Webex meetings directly from Microsoft Outlook via the official Webex Productivity Tools plugin:

  1. In Outlook Calendar, click New Meeting.
  2. In the top Outlook ribbon menu, click Add Webex Meeting or Add Personal Room.
  3. The meeting location and video link details (genericorp.webex.com/meet/yourusername) will automatically populate in the invitation body.
Webex FAQ 2: How do I share my screen, specific windows, or audio during a Webex call?

During an active meeting, click the central Share Content button (square icon with an up arrow):

  • Entire Screen: Displays everything visible on your selected monitor.
  • Specific Application Window: Hides personal notifications, desktop background, and other open apps.
  • Sharing Video/Audio: Ensure you check the box labeled "Optimize for motion and video" and enable "Share computer audio" at the top left of the share menu.
Webex FAQ 3: How do I enable virtual backgrounds, noise removal, or closed captions in Webex?

Enhance meeting clarity and privacy using built-in AI tools:

  • Virtual Backgrounds: Before joining or during a call, click Change Background on your video preview screen and select a GeneriCorp branded corporate background or Blur effect.
  • Webex Smart Audio Noise Removal: Go to Audio Settings > Webex Smart Audio and enable Webex AI Noise Removal to filter out keyboard typing, dogs, or HVAC noise.
  • Live Closed Captions: Click the CC button on the bottom left corner to display real-time speech-to-text subtitles.
Webex FAQ 4: How do I record a Webex meeting and access cloud recordings afterwards?

Only meeting hosts or co-hosts can initiate recording:

  1. Click the round red Record button in your control panel.
  2. Select Record in Cloud (recommended for automatic transcript generation).
  3. Once the meeting concludes, the MP4 recording link and automated transcript will be emailed to you within 30 minutes and available at genericorp.webex.com under Recordings.
Webex FAQ 5: What should I do if my audio or video freezes during a Webex meeting?

If you experience lag, latency, or choppy audio:

  • Switch audio connection from computer VoIP to Call Me (Webex will call your phone number directly).
  • Disable incoming video feeds by switching view to Audio Only Mode or turning off high-definition self-video.
  • Disconnect from high-bandwidth non-essential applications or test your VPN connection.

3. Software & Productivity Applications

How to save a Word document as a PDF or lock it as Read-Only for safe external sharing?

To prevent accidental modifications when distributing documents to external partners, use either PDF export or Read-Only protection:

Method 1: Exporting as PDF (Recommended)

  1. In Microsoft Word, click File > Export > Create PDF/XPS Document.
  2. Choose your file destination and click Publish.

Method 2: Making Word File Read-Only

  1. Click File > Info > Protect Document.
  2. Select Always Open Read-Only or Restrict Editing to prevent layout changes.
How to update your Google Chrome browser to the latest secure version?

GeneriCorp security policy requires web browsers to be updated regularly to apply zero-day vulnerability patches.

  1. Open Google Chrome.
  2. Click the Three Dots menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Hover over Help and click About Google Chrome.
  4. Chrome will automatically check for and download updates. Click Relaunch to finish installing.
How do I clear my web browser cache and cookies (Chrome, Edge, Safari)?

Clearing browser cache resolves 90% of web portal login loops and outdated page display issues:

Shortcut for Chrome and Edge: Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + Delete (Mac).

  • Set Time Range to All Time.
  • Check boxes for Cached images and files and Cookies and other site data.
  • Click Clear Data and restart browser.
How do I request new software or software licenses through the Service Catalog?

Unapproved software installation is restricted by endpoint management software.

To request licensed software (e.g. Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, MS Visio, Tableau):

  1. Visit IT Portal > Software Access Request.
  2. Search for the required software title and select your business justification.
  3. Once cost-center approval is confirmed, the license auto-assigns within 2 hours and appears in your Software Center for installation.
How do I fix Microsoft Outlook when it fails to send/receive or gets stuck on 'Syncing'?

When Outlook stops updating or gets stuck in offline status:

  1. Verify your Internet connection and confirm VPN status if remote.
  2. Click the Send / Receive tab at the top of Outlook and toggle Work Offline on and off.
  3. If issues persist, launch Outlook in Safe Mode by holding Ctrl while opening Outlook, or use Outlook Web Access (outlook.office.com) as a temporary workaround.
How do I restore a previous version of a file from OneDrive or SharePoint?

GeneriCorp cloud storage preserves 500 automatic historical revisions for all files:

  1. Right-click the file in Windows File Explorer or SharePoint web interface.
  2. Select Version History.
  3. Browse timestamped versions, click the three dots next to the desired historical timestamp, and select Restore.
How do I install company-approved mobile apps on my mobile device?

GeneriCorp enforces Mobile Device Management (MDM) container security:

  1. Download the Microsoft Intune Company Portal app from iOS App Store or Google Play Store.
  2. Log in using your GeneriCorp SSO credentials.
  3. Select approved apps (Outlook, Webex, Teams, Workday) from the internal corporate store to install them in an encrypted container.

4. Security, Access & Credentials

How do I reset my Windows / GeneriCorp network password?

Passwords expire every 90 days and must be at least 14 characters in length.

  1. Navigate to https://password.genericorp.com from any device.
  2. Click Self-Service Password Reset.
  3. Authenticate via Okta MFA push notification on your smartphone.
  4. Enter your new compliant password. Updates sync across Windows, Mac, Email, and VPN within 60 seconds.
How do I set up or transfer Okta / Duo Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) to a new phone?

If you purchase a new personal or corporate mobile device:

  1. Before wiping your old phone, log into genericorp.okta.com.
  2. Click your user profile name at top right > Settings > Extra Verification.
  3. Click Edit Profile, then click Add New Phone / Set Up Okta Verify to generate a QR registration code for your new phone.

Note: If you no longer have access to your old phone, contact the IT Hotline for identity verification.

How do I report a suspicious phishing email or security threat?

Do not click any embedded links or open attachments in suspicious emails.

  1. In Microsoft Outlook, select the email.
  2. Click the Report Phish button in the Outlook toolbar menu.
  3. The email will automatically be quarantined and submitted to the Cyber Security Operations Center (SOC) for analysis.
How do I connect to the GeneriCorp Corporate VPN when working remotely?

Remote workers require GlobalProtect VPN to access internal file shares and legacy applications:

  1. Launch GlobalProtect VPN client from system tray icon (bottom right).
  2. Portal address: vpn.genericorp.com.
  3. Click Connect and approve Okta MFA push on your smartphone.
What should I do if my account gets locked out after multiple failed password attempts?

Accounts automatically lock for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive incorrect attempts to protect against brute-force attacks.

You can wait 15 minutes for automatic unlock, or perform an instant unlock at password.genericorp.com by choosing Unlock My Account.

How do I request privileged or admin rights on my corporate workstation?

Permanent administrator rights are restricted due to Zero Trust security policies.

For developer tasks or custom driver setup, request temporary 24-hour admin access via MakeMeAdmin App (Windows) or Privilege Management Tool (Mac) installed in your start menu.

How do I request access to a shared inbox, distribution list, or network drive?

Submit a request via IT Portal > Access Governance > Resource Permission.

You must specify the exact folder path or email address along with business justification. Requests route automatically to the designated resource owner for approval.

5. Office Environment, Printers & Wi-Fi

How do I connect to the secure GeneriCorp Wi-Fi network in the office?

Corporate laptops automatically authenticate to GeneriCorp-Secure Wi-Fi using device certificate security.

For mobile phones or non-managed corporate devices, select SSID: GeneriCorp-Corporate and log in with your network domain credentials.

How do I map a corporate network printer on Windows or macOS?

GeneriCorp uses Follow-Me-Print Cloud Printing:

  1. Print your document and select printer queue: \\printserver\SecurePrint-Color.
  2. Walk to any physical office printer/copier.
  3. Swipe your employee badge on the card reader to release and print your job.
What should I do if a printer shows paper jam or toner replacement errors?

Floor printers are serviced daily by Facilities. If an urgent error occurs, scan the QR code sticker located on the front panel of the printer to auto-submit a service ticket to Facilities.

How do I book a desk or meeting room using the GeneriCorp Workspace Portal?

Desk reservation is mandatory prior to entering corporate office facilities:

  1. Open workspace.genericorp.com or launch the Mobile Workplace App.
  2. Select building location, floor, and date.
  3. Pick an available desk on the floorplan map and click Reserve Desk.
How do I request temporary guest Wi-Fi credentials for external visitors?

External contractors and guests can connect to GeneriCorp-Guest network.

Visitors complete a self-registration landing page with sponsor email (e.g., your email). You will receive an instant 1-click authorization prompt to grant them 24-hour guest access.

How do I report an office AV / Conference Room TV malfunction?

For urgent in-person meeting issues, tap the Help Button on the conference room touch controller panel. An AV technician will be dispatched to the room within 5 minutes.
